## About Me

Backend and platform engineer focused on distributed systems, integrations, identity, and multi-tenant products. I work mainly with Java, Spring Boot, TypeScript, GraphQL, Kafka, Redis, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Docker, Kubernetes, and AWS.

I am currently building multi-tenant education platform features at EduTrend while pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science at the University at Buffalo. Previously, I worked at miniOrange on enterprise SaaS applications across SSO/IAM, Shopify integrations, GraphQL APIs, cloud deployments, and production reliability.
